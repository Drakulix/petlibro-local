**Subject:** Feature Request: Configurable MQTT Broker

Hi Petlibro Support,

I am a Petlibro customer and a Home Assistant user. I am writing to request a small firmware feature that would make a big difference for smart home enthusiasts like me.

**Configurable MQTT broker host, port, username, and password**

Currently, Petlibro devices are hardcoded to connect to `mqtt.us.petlibro.com`. I would love a setting — even a hidden or advanced one — that lets me point my device at a local MQTT broker instead, with the ability to set the broker host, port, username, and password. This would allow local-only operation with no dependency on the Petlibro cloud, which means my devices keep working even if the cloud service changes or goes offline.

This is a small configuration change that would have an enormous impact for users who care about local control, privacy, and long-term reliability. Many other smart home devices (such as those supported by Tasmota and ESPHome) offer this, and it is a significant selling point for technically minded buyers.
